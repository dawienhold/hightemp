How to read the comparison

Model probability

The fixed 10 AM probability prevents later information from being used to choose the historical cohort. The current-model column is separate and can disqualify a live lead if the forecast has moved above 10%.

For a 2-degree Polymarket band such as 89–90°F, the model probability is P(89)+P(90), not whichever individual card bar looks cheaper.

Market probability

Polymarket US has one YES instrument. Buying NO is economically the same as selling YES. Therefore this page uses 1 − executable NO cost as the YES probability embedded in the price at which NO exposure is available.

The YES ask is shown for context but is not used to pretend a NO trade could fill at the midpoint.

Uses the existing Contract Consistency scanner’s verified Polymarket US rule mapping and book snapshot. No keys, wallet, account balance, or order endpoint are used.

WHEN A PAPER TRADE STARTS

A fresh, qualifying edge

  1. Fit each asset’s HAR-style volatility model independently.
  2. Estimate its closing-minute reference average.
  3. Require at least 5¢ of modeled edge after fees and the reserve.
  4. Recheck after at least 2.5 seconds; simulate only if the later book still qualifies.

There is no scheduled first trade. All four streams collect forecasts without requiring a trade. The paper account has one shared $30 exposure cap across BTC and ETH.

Research assumptions and limits

Five-minute scheduled collection is not continuous trading. Missed windows and missing ticks are never filled retrospectively. Historical prices fetched at installation train the first volatility model; they are not a historical trading backtest.

The price to beat is the market’s official opening reference. The closing reference is a rounded average of 60 samples from the asset’s reference index (BRTI for BTC; ETHUSD_RTI for ETH) in the final minute. A partial final minute requires every elapsed sample; otherwise the model abstains.

Student-t tails are fixed at five degrees of freedom; drift is zero. The forecast for the arithmetic average uses a local Brownian covariance approximation. The regression forecasts one-minute variance and holds that rate constant over the remaining window. Binary calibration and net performance must be measured prospectively.

Paper positions are capped across markets, with one entry per market. Settlement requires the exchange’s final resolved status and a confirmed binary settlement. Unresolved trades remain pending.

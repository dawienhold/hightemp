What this scanner does

Implication: buy YES on the broader threshold and NO on the narrower one. The proof must establish that the narrower outcome implies the broader outcome.

Disjoint outcomes: two non-overlapping bands cannot both win. A NO/NO pair therefore pays at least $1 in ordinary numeric settlement, before subtracting purchase costs and fees.

Whole range: buy YES across bands covering every temperature, or evaluate a multi-NO basket by its complete payoff table. Missing bands, overlaps, and tails are checked explicitly.

Prices: YES purchases use YES offers. NO purchases use one minus YES bids on Polymarket US. Midpoints and last-trade prices are not purchase prices.

Recheck: a later request must still show shared depth at identical prices for every leg. The stress scenario reduces that depth and adds an adverse price buffer to each leg.

Not included: live orders, atomic execution, maker queue assumptions, cross-exchange trading, weather forecasts, rainfall/TSA adapters, or any portfolio profit total.

The source-time field is transactTime. Its exact update semantics are not independently established; strict source-age checks remain enabled. An old or missing timestamp is not silently accepted.

Standard public GitHub runner · No new account or API key · Public market-data GET requests only. Scheduled runs and publication can lag. The refresh button only reloads saved data; it does not trigger a new scan.

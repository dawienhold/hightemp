How to read this page

Weather: NWS hourly forecasts and gust guidance for U.S. parks. Toronto uses the Canadian government city forecast when its hourly window is available. The default window runs from first pitch through 3½ hours later; live games show the remaining forecast window. Delays, uncertain start times, uncovered hours, and missing gusts are labeled rather than filled with guesses.

Defense: regular-season runs allowed divided by games played, ranked lowest to highest among teams with usable data. This includes pitching, fielding, ballpark and opponent effects. It is not a pure fielding score, a park-adjusted metric, or an assessment of today's starting pitcher. The statistics cutoff and sample size are shown.

Roofs: fixed roofs are labeled indoor. For retractable roofs, outdoor conditions are shown with an unverified-roof warning. Wind direction is where wind comes from, not an inferred in/out-to-centerfield direction.

Sources: MLB public schedule, venue and standings data; National Weather Service; Environment and Climate Change Canada. No paid weather subscription or account credentials are used by this add-on. Public-source terms and service limits still apply.

How the numbers are made

The page covers the current NFL week, through Monday night. Games more than 5 days away are marked Early look, because forecasts that far out are much less reliable, and they sharpen as the game gets closer. A scheduled job pulls the schedule from ESPN every three hours and checks the forecast for each outdoor stadium over the game window, from kickoff to 3½ hours later. The forecast comes from two ensembles, GFS (31 runs) and ECMWF (51 runs). Each percentage is the share of those runs that show that much rain during the game.

Any rain: at least 0.01 in during the game, the same bar the NWS uses for "chance of rain". Moderate or heavier: at least one hour at 0.10 in/hr or more. Heavy: at least one hour at 0.30 in/hr or more.

These models step every 3 hours, so a short downpour gets spread out and the heavy figure tends to read low. Domes and SoFi Stadium's fixed roof show as indoor. Retractable roofs still get a forecast because the team decides on game day whether to close it. At 34°F or colder, precipitation may be snow.

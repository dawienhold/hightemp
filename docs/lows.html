Reading these estimates

The climate day runs from midnight to midnight local standard time. During daylight saving time, that is 1 AM to 1 AM on the clock. Each card shows its exact closing time; “tomorrow” refers to the next climate date.

The forecast blends independent hourly model series over that whole window, compares recent station observations, and keeps a conservative uncertainty allowance. A real station reading caps how high the final low can be; a preliminary CLI can also supply that cap. Neither proves that a colder low cannot occur later.

Low biases are trained only against past final CLI minimums. Adjustments require eight distinct dates, are shrunk toward zero using 20 prior days, and are capped at 1.5°F. Repeated runs and additional weather models do not count as more independent days. Low forecast scores stay separate from the high forecasts.

Sources: Open-Meteo hourly and previous-run guidance, NWS and AviationWeather station reports, and NWS daily climate reports. New low forecasts are archived automatically each pass. Individual-degree probabilities may need to be summed to match your market’s bands.
